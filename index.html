Hill Station In India

Maharashtra

  • Lonavala & Khandala – Scenic viewpoints, waterfalls & historic forts
  • Mahabaleshwar – Strawberry farms, Pratapgad Fort & Arthur’s Seat
  • Matheran – Eco-friendly hill station with toy train & lush greenery
  • Panchgani – Table Land plateau, colonial charm & strawberry farms

Himachal Pradesh

  • Shimla – Colonial charm, Mall Road & scenic toy train
  • Manali – Snow-capped peaks, Solang Valley & adventure sports
  • Dalhousie – Pine valleys, Victorian architecture & Khajjiar (Mini Switzerland)
  • Dharamshala & McLeod Ganj – Tibetan culture, monasteries & Dhauladhar views

Uttarakhand

  • Nainital – Naini Lake, Snow View Point & Mall Road
  • Mussoorie – Waterfalls, cable car rides & “Queen of the Hills”
  • Auli – India’s top skiing destination & panoramic Himalayan views
  • Ranikhet – Peaceful meadows, golf courses & temple trails

Kerala

  • Munnar – Tea gardens, Eravikulam National Park & misty hills
  • Wayanad – Waterfalls, wildlife sanctuaries & spice plantations
  • Thekkady – Periyar Wildlife Sanctuary & boating experiences
  • Ponmudi – Winding roads, misty peaks & trekking trails
